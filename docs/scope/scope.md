# Scope: SOLID FITNESS, Velachery

Prepared and updated 15 November 2026. This is a proposed v1 scope for your local gym website redesign. No implementation is authorized by this document. Business details and visual direction marked unconfirmed need your input before publication.

**Build approach:** Journey, finish the visitor path from discovering the gym to requesting a trial, then support general enquiries and visits.
**Workflow:** Alpha recommended, verify the real visitor journeys before launch. Decisions about the platform, lead handling and visual system remain for design.
**Boundary:** One Velachery branch. English first is a provisional assumption. No platform, framework or hosting provider selected.

## 1. Goal and measures

Turn local visitors into trial requests and qualified WhatsApp enquiries by making the location, offer, training options and next step clear on a phone.

Primary action: **Request a Trial**. Secondary action: **Enquire on WhatsApp**. Say “free trial” only if the owner confirms it. A request is not a confirmed booking.

Measure accepted trial requests and staff confirmed WhatsApp enquiries weekly. Also track trial attendance and resulting memberships where staff can record them. Track CTA clicks separately: opening WhatsApp does not prove a message was sent. Establish a baseline during the first 30 days, then agree numeric growth targets with the owner. Do not invent a conversion uplift promise.

## 5. Visual direction

The supplied screenshots show a black and charcoal background, orange accents, white condensed uppercase display headings, a compact horizontal header and orange action buttons. The opening composition combines a large left aligned headline with an athlete image on the right. Subsequent frames show a central athlete in different exercise poses, an introduction with a gym image and statistics, program cards, social proof and transformation images, a facility gallery, trainer cards, pricing cards and a final enquiry section.

Adapt that visual language for SOLID FITNESS: a dark athletic style, orange primary actions, strong heading hierarchy, real branch photography and clear section transitions. Use four pricing cards to match your four plans, even though the reference appears to show three. The video confirms an animated athlete hero with changing dumbbell exercise poses, changes in athlete scale and framing, and hero text disappearing and returning. Treat this effect as optional pending a usable licensed asset and motion design, with a static hero fallback. Do not copy the reference brand, athlete, trainer identities, statistics, testimonials or prices. Statistics and transformation claims are conditional on verified branch content.

The supplied images and video resolve the reference request. Both depict a desktop site inside a phone recording, so mobile layouts still need original responsive design.

### Motion observations from the supplied video

| Approximate recording time | Visible behavior | Scope implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 12 seconds | The opening headline and athlete composition changes to a more central athlete view; dumbbells move between lowered and raised poses; the headline disappears and returns | Plan a coordinated hero sequence if a suitable asset is available; preserve a readable static opening state |
| 12 to 16 seconds | The recording moves from the athlete area into the introduction and transformation content | Support clear transitions into ordinary content sections |
| 16 to 23 seconds | Facility imagery and pricing cards appear as the demonstrator navigates the page | Keep these sections readable independently of animation |
| 24 to 27.5 seconds | The recording returns to the athlete and headline composition | Returning to the hero must restore a usable state |

These times describe the recording, not animation durations to implement. The demonstrator scrolls and changes direction, so the clip does not prove whether the athlete is controlled by scrolling, video playback, a frame sequence or a 3D model. Exact triggers, easing, pinning and mobile behavior remain design decisions. Do not promise an exact animation replica from this recording alone. The recording itself is reference material, not a clean website hero asset.

For v1, keep text and trial actions available without waiting for the animation. Provide a static fallback for reduced motion, failed media and constrained devices. If the full athlete effect is pursued, review asset suitability and performance before committing to it; it must not block the enquiry journey.

Design for phones first. Keep the primary CTA visible without hiding content. Any video must have a still image fallback and must not delay the enquiry path. Respect reduced motion preferences, support keyboard navigation, show visible focus and meet WCAG 2.2 AA as the design target. Avoid forced intro animations, scroll capture and decorative effects that obstruct decisions.

## 9. Release boundary

V1 is ready when the approved branch facts are consistent, visitors can request a trial or enquire from a phone, staff receive the request, and the acknowledgement accurately explains what happens next. Verify calls, directions, request failures, keyboard access and narrow screens before publishing. Unverified trainers, prices, reviews and trial promises stay unpublished.
